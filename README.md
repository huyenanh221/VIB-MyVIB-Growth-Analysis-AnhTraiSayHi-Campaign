# VIB-MyVIB-Growth-Analysis-AnhTraiSayHi-Campaign
In 2024, VIB (Vietnam International Bank) partnered as the main sponsor for the hit reality show "Anh Trai Say Hi", launching a trendy, co-branded credit/debit card line targeting Gen Z and Millennials.

While the initial marketing campaign generated a massive surge in User Acquisition (Sign-ups) driven by exclusive concert ticket pre-sales and idol-themed card designs, the product team noticed two critical issues 30 days post-show:
1. High Drop-off in the Digital Funnel: A large volume of users initiated the card application on the VIB MyVIB app but abandoned it mid-way.
2. The "Ticket Churn" Effect: Users who successfully activated the card utilized it only to purchase concert tickets, leaving the card completely inactive (dormant) afterward.
Objective: As a Product Analyst, I conducted a Funnel and Cohort Retention Analysis using SQL and Python to locate user friction points and propose data-driven features to convert show fans into long-term active banking users.
